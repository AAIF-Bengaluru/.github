Namaskara, Bengaluru! We are the Bengaluru chapter of the [Agentic AI Foundation](https://aaif.io/).
We bring developers, researchers, and builders together to share practical work in agentic AI.

**[Find events, register, and contact organizers on Luma](https://luma.com/aaif-bengaluru)**

## What to expect

- Technical talks and hands-on demos with working code and real artifacts.
- Lessons from production: what works, what fails, and what remains unsolved.
- Collaboration on open-source agentic AI projects in a vendor-neutral setting.

## Get involved

Bring a demo, propose a technical session, offer a venue, or volunteer at an event.
Use our [support routes](https://github.com/AAIF-Bengaluru/.github/blob/main/SUPPORT.md) to get started.
